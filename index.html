      How to play — Guess The Player

      1. Each day one World Cup player (2018 or 2022) is the mystery target.
      2. Guess any player; you get a % match, a pitch comparison, and coaching.
      3. Exact name + tournament wins. 6 guesses. New at midnight UTC.
      4. After 3 guesses, position hint (DEF / MID / FWD). After 5, archetype hint.
